[186] Heading 1 === ABSTRACT 
[187] Normal ===  
[188] Normal === The proliferation of misinformation, particularly through encrypted messaging platforms like WhatsApp, presents a significant socio-technical challenge in regions like India. Traditional fact-checking is manual and unscalable, while standalone AI models are prone to hallucinations and lack contextual grounding. This project, TruthSeeker, proposes and implements a novel Compound AI System that synergistically integrates three distinct technological layers: 1) A Machine Learning module for forensic stylistic analysis to detect manipulative language patterns, 2) A Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engine anchored to a curated, localized knowledge base to ensure factual grounding, and 3) A state-of-the-art Large Language Model (Grok Llama 3.1 & 3.3) for sophisticated, evidence-based reasoning and explanation generation. The system is encapsulated within a modern full-stack application featuring a React-based glassmorphic UI and a FastAPI backend. By moving beyond monolithic AI approaches, TruthSeeker achieves a critical balance between automation speed, analytical depth, and veracity, effectively identifying not just blatant falsehoods but also nuanced exaggerations in viral content, thereby offering a scalable prototype for next-generation digital literacy tools.

[203] Heading 2 === Motivation
[204] Normal === The motivation behind TruthSeeker is driven by the societal harm caused by viral misinformation, particularly sensationalist 'WhatsApp Forwards' that incite panic, distribute false medical advice, or push political propaganda. Traditional fact-checking is slow and manual; there is a critical need for an automated, skeptical AI that can verify claims in real-time.
[205] Heading 2 === Project Objective 
[206] Normal === The motivation for this project is multi-faceted, driven by both technological and societal imperatives: 
[207] Normal === Societal Impact: To combat the erosion of public trust in institutions, media, and science, and to empower individuals with tools to critically assess the information they consume. 
[208] Normal === Technological Curiosity: To explore and validate a hybrid AI architecture that mitigates the key weaknesses of standalone LLMs (like hallucination) by grounding them in retrievable facts. 
[209] Normal === Research Advancement: To contribute to the field of NLP and misinformation research by demonstrating the efficacy of combining parametric knowledge (pattern recognition) with non-parametric knowledge (RAG) for a complex classification task. 
[210] Heading 2 === Scope of the project
[211] Normal === The system accepts text inputs (such as headlines or forwarded messages) and processes them through multiple modules: stylistic analysis (ML), LLM query generation, fact-check tool lookup, and live news searching. The final aggregation is performed by a Master LLM (over Groq) and the results are displayed via a responsive web application. The scope excludes video and image forensics deep-fake detection.
[212] Normal === 
[213] Heading 2 === Related previous work
[214] Normal === Previous approaches to fake news detection relied either purely on natural language classification (e.g., training a model on the LIAR dataset) or manual fact-checking databases. While classification models can detect stylistic bias, they hallucinate facts. Recent advancements in RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) have allowed AI to consult external knowledge bases, enabling systems to ground LLM reasoning in verified facts. TruthSeeker builds upon these concepts by hybridizing stylistic ML with real-time API integrations.

[330] Heading 2 === Class diagrams
[331] Normal === The backend relies on key modular classes:
1. LLMService: Manages interactions with Groq and DuckDuckGo.
2. FactCheckService: Manages requests to Google Fact Check Explorer.
3. NewsService: Manages live news retrieval from NewsAPI/GNews.
4. PropagandaDetector: Encapsulates the Scikit-learn TF-IDF pipeline and RandomForest model.
[332] Heading 2 === Data Flow Diagram
[333] Normal === Level 0 DFD: Context Diagram
[334] Normal === The Context Diagram represents the entire TruthSeeker AI system as a single, high-level process, highlighting its interactions with external entities.
[335] Normal === Process 0.0 (TruthSeeker System): The central compound AI application that acts as the primary orchestrator.
[336] Normal === External Entity 1 (End User): The human user interacting with the React web interface.
[337] Normal === Data In: The user inputs a raw text string (Claim), representing a news headline, WhatsApp forward, or political statement.
[338] Normal === Data Out: The system outputs a compiled Verdict Dashboard Payload (JSON containing the color-coded verdict, fact-checker sources, AI explanations, and risk bars) back to the user.
[339] Normal === External Entity 2 (External APIs - Groq, Google Fact Check, NewsAPI, DDG): Third-party intelligence tools.
[340] Normal === Data In: The system sends optimized Search Queries and Prompt Packets for contextual research.
[341] Normal === Data Out: The APIs return structured JSON containing Live News Summaries, Fact-Check Debunks, and Deep LLM Analysis.
[342] Normal === Level 1 DFD: Sub-Process Breakdown
[343] Normal === The Level 1 Diagram breaks down the central TruthSeeker system (Process 0.0) into its core functional sub-processes, revealing how the Claim creates parallel streams of data analysis.
[344] Normal === Process 1.0 (Claim Validation & Routing):
[345] Normal === Input: Receives Raw Text Claim from the user.
[346] Normal === Action: Cleans the text, maps route handlers, and dispatches the text simultaneously to the ML layer and the RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) layer.
[347] Normal === Process 2.0 (Stylistic Feature Scoring):
[348] Normal === Input: Receives Cleaned Text from Process 1.0.
[349] Normal === Action: Interacts with the local propaganda_model.pkl data store (Random Forest / TF-IDF) and applies rule-based sensationalism heuristics (emoji count, caps ratio).
[350] Normal === Output: Pushes a Suspicion Risk Score (0.0 - 1.0) down the pipeline for final aggregation.
[351] Normal === Process 3.0 (Dynamic Query Generation):
[352] Normal === Input: Receives Cleaned Text.
[353] Normal === Action: Groq’s fast Llama-3.1-8b converts the raw text into three highly optimized Search Queries (Fact Query, Open Web Query, Live News Query).
[354] Normal === Process 4.0 (RAG Context Aggregation):
[355] Normal === Input: Uses the optimized queries from Process 3.0 to hit three external endpoints: Google Fact Check Tools, DuckDuckGo Search, and NewsAPI.
[356] Normal === Action: Collects live web snippets, official debunk reviews, and related recent news headlines.
[357] Normal === Output: Packages the findings into a structured Aggregated Evidence Packet.
[358] Normal === Process 5.0 (Master Verdict Synthesis):
[359] Normal === Input: Receives the original Claim, the Suspicion Risk Score (from 2.0), and the Aggregated Evidence Packet (from 4.0).
[360] Normal === Action: Compiles all inputs into a massive contextual prompt and invokes the master LLM (Groq Llama-3.3-70b). Applying deep critical reasoning, the model cross-references stylistic risk against verifiable intelligence.
[361] Normal === Output: Generates a Final Verdict Data object containing the human-readable explanation, intent analysis, and risk categorization.
[362] Normal === Process 6.0 (Format Unified Response):
[363] Normal === Input: Final verdict data.
[364] Normal === Action: Binds the AI output with the raw News and Fact-Check schemas.
[365] Normal === Output: Transmits the Verdict Dashboard JSON via REST back to the React UI for visual rendering.

[454] List Paragraph === Conclusion 
[455] Normal === This project successfully designed, implemented, and validated a hybrid fake news detection system that effectively addresses key limitations of existing approaches. By integrating a Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) pipeline with a Pattern Recognition model, the system achieves a synergistic balance between evidence-based verification and stylistic analysis. 
[456] Normal === TruthSeeker project successfully demonstrates the viability and superiority of a Compound AI approach to the complex problem of automated misinformation verification. By strategically layering a fast ML filter with a grounded RAG+LLM reasoning engine, the system effectively mitigates the core weaknesses of individual techniques—namely, the superficiality of classic ML and the hallucination tendency of pure LLMs. The developed prototype proves capable of handling the nuanced nature of real-world "WhatsApp hoaxes," including the critical task of distinguishing exaggerated claims from completely false ones. The integration into a cohesive, user-friendly full-stack application underscores the project's value as both a research artifact and a potential tool for practical deployment.

[458] List Paragraph === Future Scope 
[459] Normal === To enhance the system's robustness, scalability, and real-world applicability, the following extensions are proposed: 
[460] Normal === Multimodal Verification: Integrate modules for image and video analysis (e.g., reverse image search, detection of deepfakes, OCR for text in memes) to handle the multimodal nature of modern misinformation. 
[461] Normal === Advanced Propagation Analysis: Incorporate network/graph analysis features to track the spread of a claim, identify potential amplification bots or coordinated inauthentic behavior, and use virality patterns as an additional signal for risk assessment. 
[462] Normal === Adversarial Robustness & Continuous Learning: Implement mechanisms for continuous model retraining using new, labeled examples of deceptive content to stay ahead of evolving adversarial tactics. This could include humanin-the-loop feedback from users flagging incorrect verdicts. 
[463] Normal === Deployment & Localization: Package the system for broader deployment (e.g., as a browser extension or a Telegram/WhatsApp bot API) and localize the knowledge base and models for other linguistic and regional contexts beyond the initial focus.
